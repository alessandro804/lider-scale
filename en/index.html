What is inside

Everything a sales floor needs, without eight tools talking badly to each other.

Visual pipeline and history

Board and table, stage by stage, with every call, message and meeting on record. A rep who leaves the company does not take the conversation with them.

Proposals and contracts

A private page per client, a PDF with your brand, a version frozen at send time and signature by link with IP and timestamp.

Money per deal

Contracted, invoiced, received, pending and overdue, installment by installment. A closed deal is not money in the bank, and the system knows the difference.

Automatic commission

The rule applied is stored inside the sale. Changing the table later never recalculates what was already sold.

Targets and performance

Targets per person, per team and per product. The SDR is measured by qualified opportunity and the closer by conversion, which is what is fair for each role.

Action center

The screen a rep opens in the morning, with the day already sorted by priority, instead of a task list nobody owns.

WhatsApp built in

Proposals, reminders and follow up go out from the company number, inside business hours, and never twice with the same message to the same person.

Permission by role

Each person sees what is theirs. A rep does not see a colleague's commission or the system settings, and the manager sees the whole operation.

Who it is for

Four roles, four different screens, the same data underneath.

SDR

Finds the company, records the contact and books the meeting. Measured by qualified opportunity, not by call volume.

Closer

Gets the meeting with the context already prepared, builds the proposal on the spot and closes with signature and deposit in the same link.

Manager

Opens one screen and sees the whole operation: pipeline, targets, conversion, forecast and whatever is sitting still.

Finance

Confirms receipts, tracks installments and releases commission with the rule and the calculation base in plain sight.

Questions that always come up

How long until the system is live?

Onboarding is guided by us and the team starts using it in the first week. What takes time is not the software, it is agreeing on the rules of your operation: pipeline stages, products, targets and the commission rule.

Do I have to change the company WhatsApp number?

No. The system works with the number you already use, and messages go out with your company identity, inside business hours.

Does it work for selling services and for selling products?

It works for both. What changes is the catalog and the commission rule, and you configure both yourself, without touching code.

I use spreadsheets today. Can I migrate?

Yes, and importing your base is part of the onboarding. Leads, clients, history and open opportunities are in the system before the team starts using it.

Who owns the data?

You do. Your base is yours, kept separate from any other client, and you can export it whenever you want.

Can we start with two reps?

Yes, and that is the most common start. The monthly fee already covers four users, so the team can double before the price moves.

Do you serve companies outside Brazil?

We do. The system and the support work in English, Spanish and Portuguese, and the contract can be in US dollars or in euros.
