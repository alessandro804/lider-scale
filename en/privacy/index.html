This Privacy Policy explains how Lider Scale collects, uses, shares and protects personal data, and what your rights are, in accordance with the Brazilian General Data Protection Law (LGPD, Law 13,709/2018) and other applicable rules.

1Who we are and our role

Lider Scale is operated by Lider Commerce LTDA, registered under Brazilian company number (CNPJ) 46.480.030/0001-43, with offices at Rua Cornelio Benfica, Camel Center Building, 8th floor, suite 801, Nova Serrana, MG, Brazil.

Your account data: we are the controller

As regards registration, billing and platform usage data of your company, Lider Commerce decides the purposes and the means of processing, and is therefore the controller.

Data you enter: we are the processor

As regards personal data of third parties that you enter or import, such as contacts at prospective companies and representatives of your clients, you are the controller and Lider Commerce acts as processor, handling that data only according to your instructions and as needed to deliver the service you subscribed to.

If you were contacted by a company that uses Lider Scale and want to exercise rights over your data, the request should be addressed to that company, which is the controller. We can assist them technically, but they are the ones deciding on that processing.

2Scope of this policy

This policy applies to personal data processed through the liderscale.com website and the Lider Scale platform.

It does not apply to third-party websites and services you reach from here, each of which has its own policy.

3Data we process

Data you provide

Name, email, telephone, job title, company, company registration number or equivalent, address, billing data and the information you type into the platform's screens.

Data generated by use

IP address, date and time of access, browser and device, pages and screens visited, actions taken in the platform and error logs. This data supports security, auditing and the operation of the service.

Third-party data you enter

Name, job title, email, telephone and company of the contacts you record, the content of the proposals you build and the history of the deals you track. As regards this data, each party's role is set out in the first section of this policy.

4How we collect

We collect directly from you, at registration, at subscription and in daily use of the platform; automatically, through technical logs and cookies; and from third parties, when you connect the platform to an external service and authorise the exchange of information.

5Purposes of processing

We process personal data in order to:

  • create and maintain your account and your team's access;
  • deliver the service you subscribed to and provide the platform's features;
  • invoice, collect payment and meet tax and accounting obligations;
  • provide support and respond to your requests;
  • ensure security, prevent fraud and maintain audit records;
  • communicate changes in features, pricing and policies;
  • produce aggregated statistics to improve the platform;
  • comply with legal or regulatory obligations and exercise rights in legal proceedings.

6Legal bases

Each processing activity rests on a legal basis under the LGPD: performance of the contract and preliminary procedures; compliance with a legal or regulatory obligation; legitimate interests, for security, fraud prevention and service improvement, always balanced against your rights; the regular exercise of rights in legal proceedings; and consent, where it is requested separately, such as for sending marketing communications.

Where processing rests on consent, you may withdraw it at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out beforehand.

7Data on the customer's contacts and clients

The data you enter about contacts and clients is processed by us only to operate the platform: storing, displaying, organising, sending the proposal you asked to send and producing the indicators you configured.

We do not sell that data, we do not use it to prospect on our own account and we do not cross-reference it with another customer's data. Each account is isolated from the others.

It is up to you, as controller, to ensure a lawful basis for recording those contacts and to respond to requests made by the data subjects.

8Artificial intelligence

Some features use artificial intelligence to suggest text, summarise a deal or point to a next step. For that, the necessary content is sent to contracted model providers, under a contractual duty of confidentiality and of use restricted to the purpose.

Your account data is not used to train general-purpose models.

Suggestions are support, not decisions. Human review before use is mandatory.

9Data sharing and processors

We share personal data only where necessary, with:

  • infrastructure and hosting providers that run the platform;
  • email and messaging providers;
  • payment methods and billing issuers;
  • artificial intelligence model providers, in the features that use them;
  • accounting and legal advisers, within the limits of their roles;
  • public authorities, where there is a legal request or a court order.

We do not sell personal data, we do not pass it on for third-party advertising and we do not use it to build credit profiles.

10Cookies and similar technologies

The website uses cookies necessary for operation, such as those keeping your session and your language choice, and may use audience measurement cookies to understand what people are looking for.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking the necessary cookies may prevent parts of the platform from working.

11International transfers

Some of our processors handle data outside Brazil. In those cases, the transfer is carried out with the safeguards the LGPD requires, such as specific contractual clauses and verification of the level of protection offered by the destination country or organisation.

If you are established in the European Union or the United Kingdom, the transfer of your data to Brazil rests on the appropriate safeguards set out in the legislation applicable to you.

12Automated decisions

The platform presents automatically calculated classifications and indicators, such as an opportunity score or a team ranking. These figures support the customer's management, and the customer decides.

We do not take automated decisions producing legal effects on you or significantly affecting you. Should that change, this policy will be updated and you will be able to request a review.

13Storage and security

Data is held on servers located in Brazil, with a contracted infrastructure provider, under restricted and logged access.

We apply traffic encryption, backup encryption, isolation of each account's data, role-based access control, activity logging and a periodic backup routine.

Access by members of our team to an account's data happens only where necessary for support or maintenance, and is logged.

14Security incidents

Should a security incident occur that may give rise to relevant risk or harm, we will notify you and the Brazilian National Data Protection Authority within the deadlines and conditions of the law, describing the nature of the data affected, the risks involved and the measures taken.

15Retention periods

We keep data for as long as necessary for the purposes in this policy.

Once the account is closed, data remains available for export for 30 days and, after that period, is deleted from production environments, and from backups within the normal purge cycle.

Billing data and access logs the law requires us to keep, and data needed for the regular exercise of rights in legal proceedings, are retained for the legal period.

16Your rights as a data subject

The LGPD grants you the right to:

  • confirm that we process your data and access it;
  • correct incomplete, inaccurate or outdated data;
  • request anonymisation, blocking or deletion of unnecessary data or data processed in breach of the law;
  • request portability to another provider;
  • request deletion of data processed on the basis of your consent;
  • know who we share your data with;
  • be informed about the possibility of refusing consent and the consequences of doing so;
  • withdraw consent;
  • object to processing carried out on the basis of legitimate interests.

17How to exercise your rights

Write to contato@lidercommerce.com.br describing your request. We may ask for additional information to confirm your identity, which serves to protect you.

We respond within the period set by law. Where the request concerns data for which we are the processor, we will forward it to the responsible customer and let you know.

18Data of children and adolescents

Lider Scale is a work tool, intended for professionals, and is not directed at people under 18.

We do not knowingly collect data of children and adolescents. If we identify such processing without the required legal basis, the data will be deleted.

19Users outside Brazil

If you are outside Brazil, processing follows this policy and the LGPD, without prejudice to the rights granted to you by the legislation of your own country.

Versions of this policy in other languages are courtesy translations. In case of divergence, the Portuguese version prevails.

The Portuguese version of this policy is the binding one. This English version is provided so that you can read the document in your own language, not to replace it.

20Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated to reflect changes in features, legislation or practice. The version in force is always published on this page, with the date of the last update.

A material change is communicated at least 30 days in advance through the registered contact channels.
